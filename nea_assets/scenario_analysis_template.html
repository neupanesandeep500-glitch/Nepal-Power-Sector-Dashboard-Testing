Scenario Analysis — Power System Decision Support

Build, combine and compare multiple demand, generation, hydrology, trade and grid scenarios over a 5–20 year planning horizon. The model is intended for screening and policy/system-expansion analysis; financial results are illustrative.

Decision Year — Scrub Across the Planning Horizon

Drag the slider (or hit Play) to move the evaluation year. Every KPI, chart and table below recalculates live for the active variant at that year — use it to walk from short-term operating conditions through to the long-term strategic picture.

Decision Recommendation

A composite score combines net revenue and constraint risk across all enabled variants at the target year to suggest the strongest candidate. Use it as a starting point, not a final answer.

Sensitivity — What Moves Net Revenue?

Each driver in the active variant is nudged up/down independently and the resulting swing in target-year net revenue is measured, holding everything else fixed.

Planning-Under-Uncertainty

Assign each enabled variant a likelihood (weights need not sum to 100 — they're normalized automatically) to get a probability-weighted expected outcome across the scenario set.
