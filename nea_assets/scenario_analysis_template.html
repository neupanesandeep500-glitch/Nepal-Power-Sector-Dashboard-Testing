Scenario Analysis — Power System Decision Support

Build, combine and compare multiple demand, generation, hydrology, trade and grid scenarios over a 5–20 year planning horizon. The model is intended for screening and policy/system-expansion analysis; financial results are illustrative.

Stage 1 — Scenario Library

Start with a planning case or create several variants for side-by-side comparison.

Stage 2 — Demand & Generation

Define demand growth and the development rate of available generation. These assumptions are applied annually unless a staged year is selected later.

Macroeconomic Drivers

GDP, GDP–electricity elasticity, inflation, population/consumer growth and tourism are explicit scenario drivers.

Electrification Drivers

EVs, charging intensity, induction and efficiency are modelled separately from GDP-linked demand.

Planning principle: the base year is a reference point. Future years are forecast from the selected drivers; the first forecast year is not automatically forced equal to the base year.

Stage 7 — Advanced Simulation & User Trajectories

Build a fully user-adjusted planning case using constant assumptions, annual trajectories, deterministic stress tests and probabilistic uncertainty. All active inputs propagate through demand, peak, generation, storage, losses, imports/exports, adequacy, grid stress and planning scores.
